# Kestrel Outdoor Supply: Customer Policy

Fictional store policy used as sample data for this demo.

## Returns

You can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and include all tags and accessories.

To start a return, open the Returns page in your account, choose the order and the items, and print the prepaid return label. Returns within the EU, the United Kingdom, Norway and Switzerland are free. Pack the items securely and drop the parcel at any partner carrier location within 7 days of creating the label.

The following items cannot be returned: gift cards, personalised or engraved products, opened fuel canisters, and items marked as final sale.

## Refunds

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days after the returned items arrive at our warehouse and pass inspection. Your bank may need a further 3 to 5 business days to show the refund.

Original shipping costs are refunded only when the return is caused by our error, such as a damaged, defective or incorrect item. Express shipping upgrades are never refunded for change-of-mind returns.

If an order is cancelled before it ships, the full amount including shipping is refunded within 2 business days.

## Exchanges

We exchange items for a different size or colour of the same product at no extra cost, as long as the exchange is requested within 30 days of delivery and the replacement is in stock. Start an exchange from the Returns page and select "Exchange" instead of "Refund". The replacement ships as soon as the original item is scanned by the carrier.

For a different product, return the original item for a refund and place a new order.

## Damaged, defective or incorrect items

If an item arrives damaged, defective or is not what you ordered, report it within 7 days of delivery through the Contact page. Include your order number and clear photos of the item and the packaging.

Once approved, we send a free replacement or issue a full refund including original shipping, whichever you prefer. You do not need to return a damaged item unless we ask for it; if we do, we provide a prepaid label.

## Shipping options and costs

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days and costs 4.95 EUR. Standard shipping is free on orders over 75 EUR.

Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs 12.95 EUR. Orders placed before 14:00 (CET) on a business day ship the same day.

Every order receives a tracking number by email as soon as it leaves our warehouse. Orders usually leave the warehouse within 1 business day.

## International shipping

We ship to all EU countries, the United Kingdom, Norway and Switzerland. International standard shipping takes 5 to 8 business days and costs 9.95 EUR, free on orders over 150 EUR.

For deliveries to the United Kingdom, Norway and Switzerland, customs duties and import taxes may be charged by the destination country and are paid by the recipient. We do not currently ship outside Europe.

## Delivery delays and lost parcels

If your tracking has not updated for 5 business days, or your parcel is more than 5 business days past its estimated delivery date, contact us and we will open an investigation with the carrier.

If the carrier confirms the parcel is lost, or the investigation takes longer than 10 business days, we send a free replacement or a full refund, whichever you prefer. If tracking shows the parcel as delivered but you have not received it, check with neighbours and your building's reception first, then contact us within 14 days of the delivery date.

## Changing or cancelling an order

You can change or cancel an order within 1 hour of placing it from the Orders page in your account. After 1 hour, or once the order status is "shipped", it can no longer be changed or cancelled; you can return it after delivery instead.

The delivery address cannot be changed after an order has shipped. The carrier's redirection service may be available from the tracking page.

## Warranty

All products carry a 2-year warranty against manufacturing defects from the date of delivery. The warranty does not cover normal wear and tear, accidental damage, or damage caused by improper use or modification.

To make a warranty claim, contact us with your order number, a description of the problem and photos. We repair, replace or refund the item at our discretion.

## Price adjustments

If the price of an item you bought drops within 14 days of your order date, we refund the difference once per item. Contact us with your order number and a link to the lower price. Price adjustments do not apply to flash sales, clearance items or prices from other retailers.

## Payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay and Klarna. Your card is charged when the order is placed. We never store full card numbers.

## Contact and support hours

Customer support is available by chat and email Monday to Friday from 08:00 to 18:00 CET, and Saturday from 09:00 to 14:00 CET. We answer emails within 1 business day. The support email address is support@kestrel.example.
